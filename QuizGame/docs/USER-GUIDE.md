## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the QuizGame website at [https://quizgame.example.com](https://quizgame.example.com)
2. Click the "Register" button in the top-right corner
3. Fill out the registration form with your:
   - Email address
   - Password (at least 8 characters)
   - Display name
4. Click "Create Account"
5. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox

![Registration Screen](/public/images/docs/registration-screen.png)

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the login page by clicking "Login" in the navigation bar
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"
4. (Optional) Check "Remember Me" to stay logged in on your device

### Navigating the Dashboard

After logging in, you'll be directed to your personalized dashboard:

![Dashboard Overview](/public/images/docs/dashboard-overview.png)

The dashboard includes:

1. **Navigation Menu**: Access different sections of the application
2. **Recent Quizzes**: Quizzes you've recently taken or created
3. **Performance Statistics**: Your quiz performance metrics
4. **Recommendations**: Suggested quizzes based on your history
5. **Notifications**: System announcements and updates

### Account Settings

To update your account settings:

1. Click on your profile picture in the top-right corner
2. Select "Settings" from the dropdown menu
3. From here you can:
   - Update your profile information
   - Change your password
   - Set notification preferences
   - Change display language
   - Update your profile picture

## Student Guide

### Finding Quizzes

#### Browse Quizzes by Category

1. Click "Browse Quizzes" in the navigation menu
2. Select a category from the list
3. Use filters to narrow down results:
   - Difficulty level
   - Time required
   - Rating
   - Keywords

![Browse Quizzes Screen](/public/images/docs/browse-quizzes.png)

#### Search for Specific Quizzes

1. Click the search icon in the navigation bar
2. Enter keywords related to the quiz you're looking for
3. Press Enter or click the search button
4. Filter results as needed

### Taking a Quiz

#### Starting a Quiz

1. Click on the quiz card to view details
2. Review the quiz description, number of questions, and time limit
3. Click "Start Quiz" when ready

![Quiz Details Screen](/public/images/docs/quiz-details.png)

#### Answering Questions

1. Read the question carefully
2. Select your answer by clicking on it
3. For multiple-choice questions, only one answer can be selected
4. For multiple-selection questions, click all applicable answers
5. Click "Next" to proceed to the next question
6. The progress bar at the top shows how many questions remain

![Quiz Question Screen](/public/images/docs/quiz-question.png)

#### Quiz Timer

- If the quiz has a time limit, a timer will appear at the top of the screen
- When time runs out, your current answers will be automatically submitted

#### Submitting Your Quiz

1. After answering all questions, you'll see a review screen
2. Check for any unanswered questions (marked with an exclamation icon)
3. Click "Submit Quiz" when ready
4. Confirm submission in the dialog box

### Viewing Results

#### Immediate Results

After submitting a quiz, you'll see your results:

1. Overall score and pass/fail status
2. Breakdown of correct and incorrect answers
3. Explanations for each question (if enabled by the quiz creator)
4. Time spent on the quiz

![Quiz Results Screen](/public/images/docs/quiz-results.png)

#### Reviewing Past Attempts

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "My Attempts" in the sidebar
3. Select a quiz from your history to review your performance
4. Compare multiple attempts to track improvement

### Tracking Progress

#### Performance Analytics

1. Navigate to the "Statistics" section from your dashboard
2. View graphical representations of:
   - Overall performance across all quizzes
   - Performance by category
   - Performance trends over time
   - Areas of strength and weakness

![Student Statistics Screen](/public/images/docs/student-statistics.png)

#### Achievements and Badges

1. Click on "Achievements" in your profile
2. View badges earned for:
   - Completing quizzes
   - Achieving perfect scores
   - Consistent practice
   - Subject mastery

## Teacher Guide

### Creating a Quiz

#### Basic Quiz Setup

1. Click "Create Quiz" on your dashboard
2. Enter basic quiz information:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Category
   - Difficulty level
   - Time limit (optional)
   - Tags for searchability

![Create Quiz - Basic Info](/public/images/docs/create-quiz-basic.png)

#### Adding Questions

1. Click "Add Question"
2. Select the question type:
   - Multiple choice
   - True/False
   - Multiple selection
   - Matching
   - Short answer
3. Enter the question text
4. For math equations, use the MathJax editor by clicking the "Σ" icon
5. (Optional) Add an image by clicking "Add Image"
6. Add answer options
7. Mark the correct answer(s)
8. (Optional) Add an explanation that will be shown after answering
9. Set points value for the question
10. Click "Save Question"

![Create Quiz - Add Question](/public/images/docs/create-quiz-add-question.png)

#### Organizing Questions

1. On the question list screen, drag and drop questions to reorder them
2. Click the edit icon to modify an existing question
3. Click the duplicate icon to create a copy of a question
4. Click the delete icon to remove a question

#### Quiz Settings

1. Click "Quiz Settings" tab
2. Configure additional options:
   - Whether to randomize question order
   - Whether to show correct answers immediately
   - Passing score percentage
   - Whether to allow retries
   - Whether to make the quiz public or private

![Create Quiz - Settings](/public/images/docs/create-quiz-settings.png)

#### Publishing a Quiz

1. Preview your quiz by clicking "Preview"
2. Make any necessary adjustments
3. Click "Publish" to make the quiz available
4. Select who can access the quiz:
   - Public (anyone)
   - Private (only via direct link)
   - Specific users (enter email addresses)

### Managing Quizzes

#### Viewing Your Quizzes

1. Go to "My Quizzes" on your dashboard
2. View a list of all quizzes you've created
3. See statistics for each quiz:
   - Number of attempts
   - Average score
   - Completion rate

![My Quizzes Screen](/public/images/docs/my-quizzes.png)

#### Editing a Quiz

1. Find the quiz in "My Quizzes"
2. Click the "Edit" button
3. Make your changes
4. Click "Save Changes"

Note: Editing a published quiz will create a new version, preserving statistics for the original version.

#### Analyzing Quiz Performance

1. Click on a quiz in "My Quizzes"
2. Go to the "Analytics" tab
3. View detailed metrics:
   - Question-by-question analysis
   - Most missed questions
   - Average time spent per question
   - Performance distribution
   - Feedback from students

![Quiz Analytics Screen](/public/images/docs/quiz-analytics.png)

### Student Management

#### Viewing Student Performance

1. Go to "Students" in the navigation menu
2. View a list of students who have taken your quizzes
3. Click on a student to see their detailed performance
4. Generate performance reports by clicking "Generate Report"

#### Creating Student Groups

1. Go to "Groups" in the navigation menu
2. Click "Create Group"
3. Name the group
4. Add students by email address or from your existing students
5. Assign quizzes to the entire group at once

## Administrator Guide

### Dashboard Overview

The admin dashboard provides a high-level overview of the system:

1. User statistics
2. Quiz statistics
3. System performance metrics
4. Recent activities
5. Pending approval items

![Admin Dashboard](/public/images/docs/admin-dashboard.png)

### User Management

#### Managing Users

1. Go to "Users" in the admin menu
2. View all users in the system
3. Filter by role, activity status, or search by name/email
4. Click on a user to view their detailed profile

#### Modifying User Roles

1. Find the user in the user management screen
2. Click "Edit Role"
3. Select the appropriate role:
   - Student
   - Teacher
   - Admin
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Content Moderation

#### Reviewing Reported Content

1. Go to "Moderation" in the admin menu
2. View a list of reported quizzes or comments
3. Click on an item to review it
4. Choose an action:
   - Approve (dismiss report)
   - Edit (modify problematic content)
   - Remove (delete content)
   - Warn User (send a warning message)

#### Setting Content Guidelines

1. Go to "Settings" > "Content Guidelines"
2. Configure automatic content filters
3. Edit the public-facing content guidelines
4. Set up notification rules for potentially problematic content

### System Configuration

#### General Settings

1. Go to "Settings" > "General"
2. Configure:
   - Site name
   - Logo
   - Color scheme
   - Default language
   - Registration requirements

#### Integration Settings

1. Go to "Settings" > "Integrations"
2. Configure external service integrations:
   - Google Analytics
   - Social media sharing
   - LMS integrations
   - API access

## FAQ and Troubleshooting

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### General Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

**Q: Can I use QuizGame on my mobile device?**
A: Yes, QuizGame is fully responsive and works on all devices, including smartphones and tablets.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, all data is encrypted and stored securely. We do not share your personal information with third parties.

#### Student Questions

**Q: Can I retake a quiz to improve my score?**
A: This depends on the quiz settings. If the quiz creator has enabled retries, you will see a "Retake Quiz" button on your results page.

**Q: How are my scores calculated?**
A: Your score is calculated as the percentage of total possible points you earned. Each question may have different point values assigned by the quiz creator.

#### Teacher Questions

**Q: Can I import questions from other sources?**
A: Yes, you can import questions in CSV or XLSX format. Go to "Create Quiz" and click "Import Questions."

**Q: How can I share my quiz with specific students only?**
A: When publishing your quiz, select "Specific Users" and enter the email addresses of the students you want to share with.

### Troubleshooting

#### Login Issues

**Problem: I can't log in despite entering the correct password.**
Solution: 
1. Check if Caps Lock is enabled
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
3. Try resetting your password
4. Ensure your account has not been deactivated

#### Quiz Display Problems

**Problem: Images or mathematical equations aren't displaying properly.**
Solution:
1. Refresh the page
2. Try a different browser
3. Check your internet connection
4. Ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser

#### Performance Issues

**Problem: The application is slow or unresponsive.**
Solution:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Close unnecessary browser tabs
3. Clear your browser cache
4. Try using a different browser
5. If on mobile, ensure you have adequate free memory

### Getting Additional Help

If you encounter an issue not covered in this guide:

1. Click "Help" in the navigation menu
2. Use the search function to find articles related to your issue
3. Click "Contact Support" to submit a support ticket
4. For urgent issues, email support@quizgame.example.com
